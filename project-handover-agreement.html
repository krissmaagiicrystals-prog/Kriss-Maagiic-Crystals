Data Privacy, Security & Legal Compliance

The platform has been engineered adhering to modern e-commerce data privacy regulations and security industry standards:

  • PCI-DSS Compliance: Customer payment credentials (credit/debit cards, CVVs, UPI PINs) are processed directly via secure payment gateway modals (Razorpay / Cashfree). No sensitive financial credentials touch or reside on your web servers or database.
  • Data Encryption & Transport Security: End-to-end TLS/SSL encryption is enforced across all endpoints. Sensitive environment secrets (database URIs, API keys, tokens) are isolated in environment variables.
  • E-Commerce Consumer Regulations: Complete public policy pages have been built and linked in the footer, including Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Shipping & Delivery Policy, Refund & Cancellation Policy, and Contact Information.
  • Role-Based Administrative Access: Admin panel routes are strictly protected by server-side session authentication with Bcrypt password hashing.
  • Automated Data Backups: Database collections reside on MongoDB Atlas with continuous automated cloud snapshots.
